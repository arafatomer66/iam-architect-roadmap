> **The glossary is more useful than it looks.** Most confusion in IAM conversations is vocabulary confusion — two people using "role" to mean different things, or "entitlement" and "permission" interchangeably, and neither noticing for twenty minutes. An architect who uses terms precisely, and gently makes others do the same, resolves more disagreement than one who argues about design.
