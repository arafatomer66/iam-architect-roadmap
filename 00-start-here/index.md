# Start Here

Before you learn a single protocol, get clear on three things: **what IAM actually is**, **what an architect is actually paid to do**, and **where you currently stand**.

Skipping this is the single most common reason people spend two years on IAM certifications and still get told "you're an engineer, not an architect" in interviews.

## The one idea to take from this section

> **IAM is not a security product you install. It is a description of how your organisation works, encoded in a system that must stay true as the organisation changes.**

Every hard problem in this field comes from that sentence. Organisations change constantly — people join, move, leave, get acquired, get restructured, get outsourced. The system encoding all of that must change with them, in near-real-time, provably, for tens of thousands of people and hundreds of thousands of non-human identities, without ever granting the wrong person the wrong access or blocking the right person from doing their job.

That is the problem. Everything else is implementation detail.
